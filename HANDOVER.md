### Teammate 1: **FloodShield** (by Team MonsoonFive)

* **Project Focus:** An advanced flood prediction, early-warning, and mapping platform.
* **Key Features to Research:**
* Integrates NASA open-source historical datasets to analyze weather patterns, water levels, and hydrological risks.
* Features an interactive map visualizing real-time, recent, dangerous, and peak water levels across various monitoring stations.
* Incorporates a predictive window forecasting floods up to 15 days in advance alongside a chatbot for localized queries.



### Teammate 2: **FloodFinders** (from the "Through the Radar Looking Glass" Challenge)

* **Project Focus:** Radar remote sensing and inundation mapping in vulnerable river basins.
* **Key Features to Research:**
* Utilizes Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) data, polarization, and backscatter interpretation to track floods through cloud cover.
* Applies geospatial processing platforms like Google Earth Engine (GEE), QGIS, and SNAP for spatial data visualization.
* Focuses on data storytelling and transforming raw radar signals into actionable community disaster awareness insights.



### Teammate 3: **S21ARSB / A Flood of Ideas**

* **Project Focus:** Off-grid, solar-powered emergency communication hardware for flood-prone and coastal areas.
* **Key Features to Research:**
* Developed a compact, low-cost solar-powered device ("SOS COM") that stays in sleep mode and wakes up via satellite broadcasts.
* Interfaces with NASA and partner Earth observation satellites (GPM, Suomi-NPP, Aqua, Terra) using UHF bands.
* Transmits localized emergency alerts and weather forecasts over local FM radio bands and companion mobile apps when cellular and internet infrastructure collapses.



### Teammate 4: **Monsoon Overflow** (Global Winner, 2020 Space Apps Challenge)

* **Project Focus:** Best-in-class data utilization model for tracking excessive river discharge and basin overflow.
* **Key Features to Research:**
* Focused on the "Best Use of Data" award category criteria by optimizing how multi-satellite precipitation and streamflow inputs are processed.
* Evaluates rainfall accumulation feeds from the Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM) mission to calculate sudden basin saturation.
* Structural approaches used to handle high-latency vs. real-time Earth observation feeds during peak monsoon overflow events.



### Teammate 5: **"Everything Starts with Water" Global Challenge Frameworks**

* **Project Focus:** Educational and analytical tracking tool architectures built around the 2023 global water cycle theme.
* **Key Features to Research:**
* Mapping the complete path of water across terrestrial systems and tracking how climate change pressures alter river dynamics and freshwater availability.
* Integrating human drivers (such as agricultural abstraction and industrial usage) alongside natural climate anomalies into unified tracking dashboards.
* Storytelling UI/UX mechanics designed to convey complex hydrological data to local stakeholders and students.
